King Chandrasena, who learned this, thought the big tree was the cause of the accident, and ordered all trees in the kingdom to be cut down.
రాజవైద్యుడు ప్రసేనుడిని పరీక్షించి, మహారాజా! ప్రసేనుడు మామూలు మనిషి కావాలంటే సంజీవని మొక్క కావాలి. దానిని తెప్పించండి అన్నాడు. RaajyavaidyuDu PrasenuDini pareekshiMchi, "Mahaaraajaa! PrasenuDu maamoolu manishi kaavalante sanjeevani mokka kaavaali. daanini teppinchandi" annaaDu. The royal physician examined Prasena and said, "O Great King! If Prasena is to become a normal person, a Sanjeevani plant is needed. Please bring it."

Chandrasena became angry and said, "Don't tell me about plants. Because of them, this danger happened to my prince!" and got furious.

ఈ సృష్టిలో ఔషధ గుణాలు గల మొక్కలు, చెట్లు అనేకం ఉన్నాయి. ఇవి మనకు నీడను, ప్రాణవాయువును, ఫలాలను ఇస్తున్నాయి. ee sruShTilO vishwaadha gunaalu gala mokkalu, cheTTu anEkaM unnaayi. By cutting down such sacrificial trees, you have greatly betrayed nature. You have caused trouble to the people, he said.

You have opened my eyes. I regret the action I took. I have realized the truth. You selfless person only asked for this small wish, didn't you? he said.
